# Insights and Recommendations

## Customer Overview

The dashboard analyses approximately 93,358 unique Olist customers and R$ 15.4 million in total revenue.

The repeat-customer rate is 3.0%, indicating that only a small proportion of customers made more than one purchase. Most customers are one-time buyers.

## Customer Retention

The inactive-customer rate is 58.9%, meaning that more than half of the customer base has not made a recent purchase and may be at risk of churn.

This is a major retention concern. The business should focus on re-engaging inactive customers through personalised offers, reminders, and targeted marketing campaigns.

## Customer Segments

The largest customer segment is **Inactive One-Time Customer**, with approximately 53,000 customers. This segment represents customers who made only one purchase and have since become inactive.

Because this is the largest segment, it should be the main focus for retention and re-engagement campaigns.

## Revenue Trend

Revenue was highest in February and lowest in April. This indicates variation in customer purchasing activity and revenue generation across months.

## Recommendations

1. Create re-engagement campaigns for inactive one-time customers.
2. Offer personalised discounts or product recommendations to encourage repeat purchases.
3. Monitor the inactive-customer rate regularly to reduce churn risk.
4. Investigate the causes of the revenue decline in April and apply successful strategies from February.
5. Use the customer-segment filter to design targeted marketing strategies for each segment.
